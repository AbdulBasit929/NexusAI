## 1. What is being built, in one paragraph

Two to three **reference cases**, generated from a seed, kept on the laptop in `C:\NexusAI-Showcase\` (outside the repository, with a manifest of hashes), each holding **every kind of file the product reads today** in **the shapes of the files the team lead supplied**, with a **profile layer** (a synthetic person: name, national ID number, numbers, accounts, plates, handsets) that ties the families together, **cross-case overlaps** planted between the cases, and a **question catalogue** (hundreds of questions, every type and operation) each with a **truthful answer computed from the generator's own truth**, never from the engine. Loaded into a case database and put to the Ask engine, they measure what is correct, show what works, and expose what is missing. Nothing here uses a real person's data.

## 3. The architecture: evidence, records, profile, case, and where each family sits

```
 EVIDENCE            RECORDS (typed, per family)           PROFILE LAYER (entities)            CASE LAYER                 ANSWERS
 file, hash,   --->  calls, internet sessions, app use,    Person (golden record)              case record, name,         every number from a row;
 custody,            plate sightings, registrations,       Phone | SIM | Handset (IMEI)        members, authority         every row names its file
 retained            handsets, bank/wallet transactions,   Account (IBAN, wallet)               case-to-case links         and row; patterns are
 original            document text units, derived media    Vehicle (plate) | Org | Place       (shared strong keys)       "candidate, not a finding"
```
* **Records stay the evidence.** Nothing in the profile layer replaces a row: an answer about a person is composed from rows, each cited. Derived rows (OCR text of a scanned statement, a plate read from an image, a face detection) are never merged with ingested rows (ruling C12); they carry `source_kind`, a confidence and a review state.
* **The profile layer is an entity graph with provenance** (reference model: FollowTheMoney, the open investigation data model of OCCRP's Aleph: people, accounts, payments and documents as entities, **a relationship such as a payment is itself an entity with its own properties and dates**, and entities of different datasets are cross-referenced, [Aleph key terms](https://docs.aleph.occrp.org/users/getting-started/key-terms)). Here: `entity` and `relationship` and `entity_mention` exist since migration 014 (empty; `record_entities` already holds the generic-path mentions); this programme fills them.
  * **Identifiers carry the link, in three strengths.** *Strong keys* (exact, auditable): national ID number, IBAN or account number, IMEI, plate in its search key, and a mobile number **inside a registration period** (a number is re-issued: a link by number is valid-time, never timeless). *Medium keys* (propose, an analyst confirms): the same name and the same date of birth; a registered holder named in two sources. *Weak keys* (never link): a name alone, a common first name, a coincidence of one cell site.
  * **Resolution is rules, not a model; a merge is a recorded, undoable decision.** An entity is a *golden record that keeps every source statement* ("registered per source X as person P from T1 to T2"); a conflict is shown with both sources, never averaged. Each link stores its rule, its rows and who confirmed it (the provenance model of W3C PROV in spirit). Measures: **false-merge rate** (the one that matters: a false merge accuses a real person), analyst-overturn rate, missed-link rate; the generator plants decoys (same name, different CNIC; one number of two holders in two periods) so that a false merge shows.
  * **Identity data stays dark until the masking design is approved (R9).** Today names, national ID numbers and addresses are recognised, counted and dropped at ingest (migration 012 withholds the table). The profile layer therefore comes in two builds: **(a) identifier profiles** (a profile is a set of strong identifiers with no name: "profile P1: numbers X, Y; account A; plate T"), buildable now; **(b) named profiles** (name and national ID shown, masked by case policy, per-user authority), after R9 and the authority record (P5). The reference cases generate the truth for both, so (b) is measurable the day it is allowed.
* **Cross-case links.** Within a tenant and with the authority to see both cases, an entity key that occurs in two cases yields a **cross-case occurrence** ("this account also occurs in case Meridian, 3 transactions"), shown as a lead with both sources; nothing merges across cases without an analyst's confirmation, and a case that the user may not see is never named. The reference cases plant such overlaps (section 5.3) and the oracle lists them.
* **Media (images, audio, faces), later, on the same rails.** A media file is evidence; the worker derives artifacts (text by OCR, plates by ANPR, speech by ASR, faces by detection); each derived artifact is a *mention* with a confidence and a review state, linked to a profile **only by a strong key it carries** (a plate, a number spoken or written, a CNIC read by OCR) or by an analyst's confirmation. A face match is a **candidate**, never an identity, and stays behind the policy gate (D-faces). The reference cases include the synthetic inputs for each (generated statement images, plate images from the media programme's generators, text-to-speech clips are NOT generated: no consented voices; they come from the public datasets of the media programme).

## 4. Financial statements: what Pakistani statements look like, and how each format is read

**Research (sources, with what they say; none of the personal statements circulating on document-sharing sites is used: they are real people's records without a licence).**
* [ABL, "How to read a bank statement"](https://www.abl.com/how-to-read-bank-statement-pakistan/): columns of originator or beneficiary, the other bank, **debit, credit and a running balance**; the **particulars or narration** column is the most informative; a **channel** is shown (IBFT, ATM, POS, 1LINK); **the closing balance of one statement is the opening balance of the next**.
* Wallet statements (Easypaisa, from third-party samples, unverified): header with account holder, account number, IBAN and date issued; rows of date and time, a transaction detail (Bank Transfer, Money Transfer, Raast Payment, Bundles, Easyload) with the counterparty and a masked number, a channel tag (app, USSD), and balance columns **opening, incoming, outgoing, closing**, outgoing shown in **parentheses**; a transaction id, tax, fee and discount block. JazzCash and the individual banks' layouts were not found: a real sample is needed for each (decision D9).
* Not found: official layout specifications. The layouts here are therefore **drafts designed from these descriptions**, promoted only when a real, lawfully provided sample is read in the vault.

**The formats, and the reading route for each (the route decides what can be claimed):**
| Format | How it arrives | Reading route | What it can claim | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CSV, XLSX export | a table | typed ingest, layouts `bank-stmt-t1`, `wallet-t1` | rows as stated, running-balance check | **built and measured (G1 and G3 on the 8 development cases pass)** |
| **Text PDF** (an e-statement) | native text | `pdftotext -layout` (already in the worker image) then a **column parser driven by the header positions** of a layout, then the same interpreter and typed writer | the same, plus page and line of each row; opening and closing balances; continuity with the next statement | **S4** |
| **Scanned or photographed PDF, or image** | pixels | OCR (the live OCR role), then the same column parser, rows marked `ocr_derived` and `review_state unreviewed`; a row whose amount or balance does not reconcile is flagged, never silently kept | "the OCR read this", with confidence; a reconciling balance raises trust, a break lowers it | **S5** (needs an owner go to deploy the worker route) |
| **Word (DOCX)** | a table in a document | native DOCX table reader (the document pipeline reads DOCX text; its tables are a small addition) | as CSV | **S4** |
| Statement integrity | any | checks as **observations**: running balance, opening against the previous closing, duplicate pages, impossible dates, PDF producer and creation time against the statement date, font and spacing breaks | "this does not reconcile", never "forged" | **S5** |

The statements are then **linked to the profile by the strong keys they carry**: the account number or IBAN (and the holder named in the header, which stays masked by policy), the counterparty accounts and wallet numbers, and the mobile number of a wallet, which is the same number as in the call records.

## 6. The question catalogue and its oracle (every family, every operation)

One catalogue, grouped by **family** and by **operation**, each question written in several phrasings (seed-chosen, wording never tuned), each with an **oracle answer computed from the generator's truth** by code that shares nothing with the views, the interpreter or the engine, each tagged with its judge kind (scalar, set, tuples, ordered, windows, cells, path, plates, sims, statuses, models, absent, probe, refuse):
* **Calls and messages:** counts, durations, first and last, by direction, by period, longest, top-N contacts, contacts in common, hubs and services excluded, silent numbers, daily rhythm, busiest hours.
* **Internet and applications:** sessions of an address and port at a time (translation), data volume by direction, applications used, top hosts, apps by province.
* **Places and movement:** cell visits, cells of a number, co-location at the hour and at 15 minutes, possible meetings, successors, anchors, trips, impossible speeds.
* **Devices and SIMs:** handsets of a number, numbers of a handset, SIM swaps, registrations at a date, registrations in force, numbers active after deactivation, unregistered numbers, conflicting sources, makes and models.
* **Vehicles:** sightings of a plate, cameras, plates at two cameras, look-alikes, absences.
* **Money:** totals in and out, largest, counterparties, shared counterparties, pass-through, amounts under a limit, balance on a date, wallet transfers between numbers, statement reconciliation (breaks).
* **Documents:** mentions of a number, an account, a plate, a place in a document; which documents mention both of two entities; the exact quotation with its page.
* **Profiles and cross-family:** everything one profile has (numbers, accounts, plates, handsets) with each fact's source; the calls between two profiles; the payments between two profiles that also called each other that day; the timeline of a profile across families.
* **Cross-case:** which entities of this case occur in another case; the evidence on both sides.
* **Controls and probes:** absences (a number the case does not hold), premise corrections ("proves", "owns", "laundering"), refusals (the red team), question-back (a limit without an amount), declines (a family the case does not hold).
Target: **at least 400 questions** over Orion, Meridian and Control, every operation of the engine covered at least three times, controls at least 15 percent. The catalogue is written first, **gates are pre-registered**, and each stage names the run that measures it.

## 10. Stage S3, text documents and the mention index: design, truth and gates (written 2026-10-28 BEFORE any build or measurement)

**Owner's decisions (2026-10-28):** the names policy of section 9.6 is accepted (common Pakistani names in the local showcase files, always with an invalid national ID number, the SPECIMEN or SYNTHETIC line, never in the repository, the live service or a screenshot sent outside); the documents stage starts now.

### 10.1 What is built in this sub-stage, and what is not
**Built here (S3a):** (1) a **mention extractor** in the ingestion package (`ingestion/forensic_records/mention_extractor.py`, rules only, no model): it reads the passages that the existing document pipeline already produces (TXT, DOCX; PDF in S4) and returns, for each passage, the strong identifiers and places it contains, each with its normalised value, the text as written, its position, a confidence tier and the rule that found it. (2) A **document generator** (`tools/case_generator/doc_case.py`, its own random stream, outside the default build so the register is unchanged) that writes about 14 documents for a case from the planted truth, in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, with a **mention truth** (what was put where, as written) and **decoys** (text that looks like an identifier and is not). (3) A **pure-Python DOCX writer** (paragraphs, a table, right-to-left Urdu paragraphs). (4) The measurement of the extractor against the truth.
**Not built here (S3b, next):** the database side (an `entity` row per identifier, `entity_mention` rows, an analyst view of documents and their mentions, migration, ops scripts), the engine's document family (catalogue, retrieval, examples), name recognition (names stay dark until R9, and need a model), `.eml` and chat-export readers in the document pipeline, PDF documents (S4), scans (S5). Until S3b exists the document questions of the catalogue carry an oracle answer and are marked `engine: pending`; they are not put to the engine.

### 10.3 Gates (pre-registered; applied once; nothing is tuned on a case that is then judged)
* **Development:** cases 1 to 8 of the register (documents generated for each by the same generator) may be used to tune the extractor.
* **Judgement:** `orion-lahore` (seed 5001) and one fresh case (seed 5003, generated once for this) are judged **once**, after the extractor is frozen. If a gate fails, the cause is fixed and a second fresh case (seed 5004) is judged; the first result is reported.
* **G-M1 strong keys** (mobile number, national ID number, account, ICCID, IMEI): **recall at least 98 percent and precision at least 98 percent**, counted per kind on (document, normalised value) pairs, and **zero extractions of a planted decoy**.
* **G-M2 plates:** recall at least 90 percent, precision at least 95 percent (a plate is found with a cue word near it; a plate-shaped legal token is a decoy).
* **G-M3 places** (the gazetteer of the case city's areas, Latin and Urdu spelling): recall at least 90 percent, precision at least 95 percent.
* **G-M4 forms and scripts:** the same gates hold separately for documents in Urdu digits and for the UTF-16 file; a gate met only in English does not count.
* **G-M5 control document:** zero mentions in D14.
* **G-M6 linking:** every extracted mobile number, account, national ID number and plate that the truth ties to a person or a record of another family is linked by its normalised key to that same record (calls, registrations, statements, camera table) with no false link; reported as a count, gate = zero false links.
* **G-M7 safety:** the repository scanner passes on the generator and extractor; no document in the showcase contains a national ID number outside the 00000 prefix; the privacy audit finds no real-form identifier.
Latency is reported, not a gate.
