# NexusAI team-lead v2 demo plan

Status: NX-UX1A/B/C/D/E/F/G shell, Data, unified-detail, media, Ask, Activity,
Home, analyst design system and final product acceptance pass; NX-UX1 is
closed and NX-A1 is next. The full all-family story remains planned
for NX-B2. Use only capabilities that have passed their stated product-baseline
or A-certification gate. No source-only NX-UX1 change is represented as live.

## Story

Use one coherent Investigation Workspace containing representative CDR, IPDR,
subscriber/tower/device, financial, security/log, generic structured,
TXT/PDF/DOCX/OCR, image, ANPR image, video, audio, and KB/retrieval evidence.
Every family follows the same open evidence → findings → Ask → accurate answer →
source → Activity flow. The demonstration presents outcomes, not processors,
agents, models, templates, or protocol architecture.

1. Open the neutral **Investigation Workspace** and select a workspace; show
   readiness, immutable evidence, custody, and current
   processing without exposing administrative plumbing.
2. Ask a certified CDR question in plain English, then a Roman-Urdu/Urdu
   paraphrase; show the same deterministic facts and exact citations.
3. Continue with a scoped follow-up and show that case/evidence scope is retained.
4. Open a positive ANPR result and its source-time citation.
5. Show a grouped video analysis with zero plates as successful
   `complete_zero_results`, not failure or generic completion.
6. Show a deliberate unavailable/not-processed example and explain the limit.
7. Open technical disclosure to show operation/version, Fact Packet, model role,
   timings, and audit correlation—then return to the simple analyst view.
8. Finish with Activity reopen, export/provenance, and the certification matrix.

## Claims allowed

- Deterministic-first, case-scoped answers with provenance.
- Certified CDR core and evidence/package operations.
- Limited capabilities only when explicitly labeled limited/candidate.
- Local inference and protocol infrastructure without claiming that LocalAI
  provides forensic policy or that gallery models are installed.

## Claims forbidden

Do not claim universal family certification, semantic LocalAI v4.9 equivalence,
automatic identity/ownership, exact face identity, complete media understanding,
or production readiness before NX-10. Do not use the UUID-misrouting path or
typed-state paths until NX-1 live certification.
