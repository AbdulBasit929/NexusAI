# STIM phases, gates, and issue triage

- STIM-0: governance, skill, domain truth, inventories, traces, baselines, maturity matrix, issues, and exact STIM-1 scope.
- STIM-1: multi-schema intake, schema profiling, mapping, source-field preservation, quality accounting, canonical-equivalence fixtures, and bounded P1 intake fixes, starting with CDR.
- STIM-2: canonical family contracts, versioned normalization, dynamic attributes, identifier/time/event/relationship semantics, and quality states.
- STIM-3: independent analytical truth certification for important structured operations.
- STIM-4: multi-source, multi-CDR, cross-family correlation, and explicit relationship certainty.
- STIM-5: realistic multilingual query intelligence, Fact Packets, grounded narrative synthesis, validation, deterministic fallback, and latency policy.
- STIM-6: premium analyst presentation, citations, productivity, localization, accessibility, and performance.
- STIM-7: final structured-intelligence acceptance with no false coverage claims.

After internally consistent STIM-0 with no open P0, enter only a bounded STIM-1 foundation slice. Do not jump to later phases or broad UI/media redesign.

## Triage

Record issue ID, severity, evidence, component/family, analyst/correctness/security impact, active-phase relevance, decision/reason, target phase, acceptance condition, and status.

- P0: isolation/auth bypass, corruption/destructive mutation, arbitrary execution, or material silent falsification. Stop and fix immediately.
- P1: wrong classification/mapping/normalization/target/time/direction/result/relationship/citation, model-altered facts, misleading presentation, or foundational failure. Fix in the active relevant slice.
- P2: important non-blocking UX/performance/maintainability/capability. Fix now only when small, adjacent, and low risk; otherwise schedule.
- P3: cosmetic polish and convenience. Backlog.

Acceptance requires exact evidence-backed claims, zero open P0/P1 for the slice, appropriate regression levels, truthful limitations, security/scope preservation, measured performance where relevant, updated living artifacts, and separate approval for runtime or retained-state actions.
