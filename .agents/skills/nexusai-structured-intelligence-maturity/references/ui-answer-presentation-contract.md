# Analyst UI and answer presentation

Keep primary analyst navigation simple: Home, Data, Ask NexusAI, and History. Keep account/admin/advanced controls separate; do not expose model, agent, operation, family, or planner selectors to ordinary analysts.

Data should answer what was uploaded, recognized, accepted/rejected/duplicated/ambiguous, mapped/preserved, covered in time/entities, made queryable, and warrants investigation. Use friendly names, family/status/count/range/quality/capabilities, with Overview, Fields, Quality, Records, Coverage/Relationships, and Technical disclosure.

Compile answers by operation/result shape. Lead with a fact-backed direct answer and render only useful findings, metrics, comparison, relationship, timeline, table, map, evidence, limitations, and suggested questions. Do not use generic success prose or decorative findings.

Distinguish exact matches, observed associations, overlaps, candidate correlations, and conflicts. Distinguish actual evidence events, derived aggregates, and annotations. Show maps only for legitimate location evidence with time, reference, coordinates, datum, uncertainty, and source.

Citation detail may reveal source, locator, evidence/version, canonical and original values, mapping provenance, and aggregate contribution role, but not raw filesystem paths. Label representative evidence separately from complete contribution proof.

Support English, Roman Urdu input, Urdu input and RTL display without translating identifiers. Validate 390, 820, 1024, and 1440 widths, themes, long/short/streaming answers, tables, timelines, citations and History; prevent page overflow. Preserve keyboard access, focus, headings, labels, contrast, screen-reader semantics, and non-color status cues.
