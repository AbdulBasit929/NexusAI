# Multi-source and cross-family correlation

Do not treat multiple CDRs as one anonymous concatenated table. Every canonical record retains workspace/case, evidence, version, source, and locator/row.

Mature controlled operations for shared and unique contacts, reciprocal and one-way communication, frequency/duration/direction comparison, shared device/SIM/tower observations, temporal overlap, same event windows, cross-source duplicates and contradictions, first/last interaction, source-by-source comparison, and candidate multi-hop relationships.

Build an independent golden pack with known shared/unique contacts, devices, SIMs, towers, overlapping/non-overlapping times, duplicates, conflicts, and ties. State deduplication and contradiction semantics explicitly.

Cross-family joins must preserve identifier types, temporal validity, source scope, relationship strength, missing/unmatched references, and uncertainty. Graph edges must expose type, supporting operation, count/value, time/window, source/evidence, and semantic strength. Visual prominence must not imply guilt, ownership, identity, or certainty.
